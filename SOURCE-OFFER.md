# Source offer for historical binaries

Myne distributed historical release binaries under **AGPL-3.0-only**. Those
binaries are no longer offered for download, and this repository publishes no
tagged releases.

If you received a Myne binary and need its complete corresponding source,
write to **source@myne.md** with the version and platform of the binary. We
will provide the corresponding source for that binary at no charge, as
required by AGPL-3.0 section 6. This offer is valid for at least three years
from the date the binary was distributed to you.

This offer covers binaries distributed by the Myne project from its own
download origin. It does not cover a build made by a third party from modified
source; ask whoever distributed that build.
