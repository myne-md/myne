# Trademark

The Myne name, the Myne "M" mark, the wordmark and the app icon (together, the **Marks**) are
**not** covered by this repository's licenses. The code is AGPL-3.0 and the specifications are
CC-BY-4.0 (see [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md)); the Marks are neither. They are common-law
trademarks asserted through use, and the full policy lives in the branding companion repo:
[`myne-branding/TRADEMARK.md`](https://github.com/myne-md/myne-branding/blob/develop/TRADEMARK.md).

**That file is the policy. This one is a pointer**, deliberately: the Marks are versioned beside
their source art and their `LICENSING.md`, so a second copy of the rules here would be a second
thing to keep in step, and the half that drifted would be the one a fork reads.

## What the AGPL does and does not give you

The AGPL grants you the **code**: use, study, modify, redistribute, including a fork. It grants
you nothing about the **name**. A fork that presents itself as Myne, or a product named
`Myne Something`, is using the Marks without permission even though its code is lawfully derived.

Referential use needs no one's permission: an article, a review, a tutorial, a comparison, or a
sentence such as "imported from Myne" is exactly what the Marks are for.

## Where the Marks appear in this repository

- `public/myne-mark.svg`, `myne-agents/docs/design/assets/myne-mark.svg`, `myne-agents/docs/design/assets/myne-wordmark.svg`
- `.github/banner.png` / `.github/banner.gif`
- `mobile/src-tauri/icons/` and `src-tauri/icons/` (the app icon in every shipped bundle)
- the About panel, which renders the mark and the wordmark
- `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/`, the store-listing artwork

Every one of those paths is outside the AGPL-3.0 and CC-BY-4.0 grants, which is why
[`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md) names `myne-branding` as multi-licensed by folder rather than
listing the Marks under a code licence.
